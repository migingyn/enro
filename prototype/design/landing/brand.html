01 · Essence

The friend who's always early.

enro gets you out the door on time. It knows how long you really need to get ready, calls you at the right moment, and talks you through it like a person would. Not another notification.

01

Proactive

enro reaches out first. It decides when to interrupt you, not just when your trip starts.

02

Conversational

You can say "give me five minutes" and get an honest answer about whether that works.

03

Personal

It learns your habits, like running 12 minutes late leaving home, and plans around them.

En route. On time.

Our promise and our tagline. Always written with two full stops, sentence case.

02 · Logo

The wordmark and the notched o.

The o is a clock face with a slice of time cut out: the minutes enro gives back to you. The notch always points to the lower left, toward the r, as if the hand is just about to move.

05 · Texture & motion

Grain, rings and one slow sweep.

A fine film grain sits over every surface so the blue never looks flat. The radar sweep is our signature motion: one beam, one full turn every 12 seconds, clockwise, like a second hand that's in no hurry.

enr

Motion rules

  • One sweep per screen, centered on the main content.
  • Always 12 seconds per turn, linear, clockwise.
  • The sweep passes behind content, never over text.
  • Rings are 1px white lines at about 50% opacity.
  • When reduced motion is on, the sweep stays still.
  • Buttons ease the arrow 2px up-right on hover. Nothing bounces.

06 · Voice & tone

Calm, honest, a little warm.

enro sounds like a friend who checked the traffic for you. It tells you the real consequence, never guilt-trips, and adapts its tone to how much is at stake.

Casual

Coffee, dinners, lectures

Hey! Coffee with Maya is at 10. If you start getting ready now, you can walk over and still grab a seat.
Give me five minutes
Sure, that works. I'll call again at 9:40.
Serious

Interviews, meetings, appointments

Your interview is at 2:00. You usually need 45 minutes to get ready for this kind of thing, so now is a good time to start.
Can I leave at 1:40?
You'd arrive around 2:08. I'd leave by 1:32 to walk in calm.
Urgent

Flights, trains, hard deadlines

Time to go. Your flight boards at 6:35 and security is busy this morning. If you leave in the next 10 minutes, you're fine.
I'll stay on until I see you're on your way.

We say

  • "You'll miss the first 15 minutes."
  • "That works." / "That's cutting it close."
  • Real times: "Leave by 9:12"
  • Short sentences, contractions, sentence case

We don't say

  • "You're late again!" or anything that shames
  • "Reminder: event in 30 minutes"
  • Vague times: "soon", "in a bit"
  • Exclamation marks in urgent moments
