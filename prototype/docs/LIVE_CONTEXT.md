# Live location and phone pose

Open `/console.html#whereabouts`. The authenticated state API exposes `pose`, `location`, and `agentContext`; wake/departure call greetings use the live location summary when GPS is available. No call is placed by viewing the panel or enabling GPS.

Orientation and gyroscope use [Sensor Logger's documented quaternion and XYZ schema](https://github.com/tszheichoi/awesome-sensor-logger/blob/main/PUSHING.md). The console displays quaternion rotation from a recent baseline, rotation rate in rad/s, and phone angle relative to horizontal from gravity. Device pose does not establish the person's posture. Stale readings remain unknown.

`preferences.locationEnabled` is off by default. The user requested GPS context and enabled Location and Precise Location on October 24. Real fixes then arrived with approximately 6 to 12 metres of reported horizontal accuracy. Native orientation, gyroscope, and phone pose also passed live ingestion. This verifies transport and the displayed readings; it does not validate travel progress inference or the revised wake classifier.

The place reference is editable in the panel. The initial Crossroads reference uses an approximate [public project site coordinate](https://ceqanet.lci.ca.gov/2011111067/2), with an experimental 300-metre area. The private preference stores its center/radius. It is not an exact apartment boundary. Existing plans use it only when their origin matches the configured home landmark and no explicit plan coordinates were supplied.

GPS context distinguishes near the reference, repeated fixes remaining near it, movement within the area, outside the area, moving away, and uncertainty. Being outside requires three fresh accurate fixes spanning ten seconds. Remaining near the reference requires repeated inside fixes for at least fifteen seconds; this describes the area, not physical immobility. Movement uses speed with its reported uncertainty or displacement beyond both fixes' error radii. GPS alone never establishes the intended route or arrival.

Live GPS coordinates and recent fixes remain in memory for at most 45 seconds; disabling collection clears them. They are exposed only through the authenticated console/state API, never written into call evidence or audit messages. The local SVG diagram uses no external map tiles or location lookups. Invalid Location records expose bounded field names and rejection reasons for diagnosis, without raw coordinate values in diagnostics. Microphone/camera data is ignored.

Validation: 126 tests, typecheck, build, and browser JavaScript syntax checks passed for this feature. Isolated tests cover pose geometry, stale and replayed readings, GPS quality, speed uncertainty, device/session isolation, movement versus geofence membership, authenticated integration, and absence of raw GPS in persisted call state. No additional physical calls were placed during the presentation.

## Destination aware departure agent

The protected state now includes `trips`, a per plan assessment using the active phone recording's fresh GPS. A plan can carry explicit origin/destination landmarks and geofence radii. The agent distinguishes remaining near the origin, moving within that area, leaving the origin without a known direction, decreasing distance to the destination, increasing distance, and repeated fixes near the destination. Approaching is an inference from GPS distance, not verified adherence to a road route. Missing, imprecise, stale, duplicate, or mixed session evidence cannot establish arrival.

Signed voice turns refresh this assessment rather than reading a location snapshot captured when dialing. Phone orientation and Activity are reported separately; they cannot prove sitting, sleep, clothing, or departure. Saying "check my progress" requests another live check. Saying "I am leaving" or "I am walking to the parking structure" ends the reminder as **user reported departure**, retaining the GPS assessment without converting the statement into verified travel.

The scheduler still calls when movement or direction is uncertain. It suppresses a due reminder when repeated fresh accurate GPS fixes place the phone within the destination area. A leave reminder is also satisfied after the phone has left the origin and made at least 75 meters of net GPS progress toward the destination beyond the combined accuracy radii, using at least three fixes over ten seconds with the latest no older than ten seconds. Small movements, movement within the origin, and movement away from the destination do not suppress it. The satisfied job is durable and is not automatically retried if travel later stops. This confirms a device's area, not the owner's attendance. Plans can select departure only reminders to avoid a wake up call for an already dressed afternoon trip. An immediate authenticated rehearsal call is separate from the scheduled job.

For the reversed Price Center → Crossroads rehearsal, the email invitation is explicitly staged in the console, while GPS, pose, call transport, and the departure reminder are live. No email was sent or claimed as imported. An explicit `Dress code: Business professional (full suit)` line becomes a spoken preparation reminder. The parser also applies to imported structured invitation descriptions and Google Calendar descriptions; it does not infer clothing from sensors.

An explicitly selected OSRM driving adapter supports the prototype without a Google Routes key. It uses a real provider duration and distance, requires resolved landmarks, labels the absence of live traffic, and does not substitute driving for walking/transit. Assumed time to reach the car is displayed separately and subtracted alongside the arrival buffer. OSRM requests send the chosen landmark coordinates to its public routing service, not a continuous live location trail. Rate is limited to at most one real request per second, with OpenStreetMap/OSRM/FOSSGIS attribution and map correction links in the plan. See the [service usage policy](https://routing.openstreetmap.de/about.html) and [API](https://project-osrm.org/docs/v26.4.0/http).

Raw live GPS remains in memory. Chosen plan landmark coordinates are stored as private plan configuration. Call history stores a bounded semantic trip assessment, including status, summary, pose, Activity, observation time, and distance to the destination, without raw phone coordinates or a trail.

Verification for this change: the full software suite passed 138 tests, the TypeScript build passed, and the JavaScript console syntax check passed. The live rehearsal was answered, streamed GPS/pose during the call, and reached a `moving-near-origin` assessment. It ended at the bounded time limit without verified departure. The phone's Activity later reported running; this is an OS label, not a physical trial ground truth. The true shake versus walk acceptance trial remains open.

Indoor origin refinement: GPS accuracy worse than 25 m cannot establish travel progress or arrival. If a fresh fix no older than ten seconds has uncertainty at most 100 m and its entire uncertainty circle fits inside the origin boundary, the agent can still report that the phone is within that area, explicitly quoting the uncertainty. This does not satisfy or suppress a leave reminder. Boundary overlapping fixes remain unknown. The focused trip suite passed after this refinement, and the build passed.
