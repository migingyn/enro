02 / CONNECTIONS

Phone motion

Not streaming
  1. Install Sensor Logger ↗ on your phone.
  2. Enable Pedometer, Activity, Gravity, and Accelerometer (gravity removed). Keep microphone and camera off. GPS recording is optional and stays off unless you choose to enable it.
  3. In Logger settings, enable HTTP Push, paste the private URL below, and keep the default one-second interval.
  4. Start recording and wait for a Pedometer baseline. After answering the test call, walk with the phone until six new counter increments and fresh walking Activity arrive, then say “I am up.” Check that readings continue while the screen is locked.

Keep this URL private. Background location permission can keep Sensor Logger running while GPS recording remains off. Activity readings with an unrecognized format remain unknown. Phone pickup, orientation, and acceleration do not confirm walking or departure.
