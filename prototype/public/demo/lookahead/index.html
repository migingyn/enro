What is being simulated?

2,048 possible mornings, sampled from illustrative ranges for starting, getting ready, travel, and the walk to the destination. The selected scene supplies the inputs. Forecasts are calculated locally; these inputs are not live sensor readings.

The graph illustrates those factors and sampled paths. Its dots are not autonomous agents. Probabilities describe the chosen assumptions, not measured predictive accuracy.

All scenes compare alternatives at 8:05 AM for a 9:00 AM commitment. Playback is a staged comparison, not a running clock.
